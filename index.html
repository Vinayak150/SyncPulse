Educational Rule Explainer (Non-Official Reference) Educational Aid Only
LBW & "Umpire's Call"

Under ICC playing conditions, if ball-tracking projects marginal impact or wicket contact within the Umpire's Call zone, the on-field decision stands (`Out` stays `Out`; `Not Out` stays `Not Out`), and the reviewing team retains its review.

DLS Resource Concept

Duckworth-Lewis-Stern adjusts targets using remaining overs and wickets in hand as combined scoring resources. Losing wickets before a rain stoppage sharply raises the par score required.

Free Hit & Over-Rate Rules

Under ICC Clause 21.19, a no-ball triggers a Free Hit (only run-out dismissals apply, and the Free Hit carries over if the bowler bowls a wide/no-ball). Behind-the-clock bowling sides lose 1 boundary rider (max 4 outside circle).
